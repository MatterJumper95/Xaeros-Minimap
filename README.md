## 📖 What Is Xaero's Minimap?

**Xaero's Minimap** is a client-side mod that displays a **customizable minimap** in the corner of your Minecraft screen. Unlike other minimap mods, it preserves the **vanilla Minecraft aesthetic** — clean, simple, and seamless.

Whether you're trying to find your base, locate a mining spot, or just avoid getting lost, Xaero's Minimap gives you a **crystal-clear view of your world** at a glance.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What is Xaero's Minimap?**
A: A client-side mod that displays a customizable minimap in the corner of your screen, showing terrain, players, mobs, and waypoints.

**Q: Is Xaero's Minimap free?**
A: Yes — 100% free. No key, no login, no account.

**Q: How do I create a waypoint?**
A: Press **B** (default) to create a waypoint at your current location.

**Q: How do I open settings?**
A: Press **Y** (default) to open the settings menu. You can also right-click the minimap for quick options.

**Q: How do I open the world map?**
A: Install **Xaero's World Map** and press **M** (default).

**Q: Does it work on servers?**
A: Yes — it's client-side. However, some servers restrict entity radar. Check your server's rules.

**Q: Will I get banned?**
A: No. It only displays information you can naturally see. Check server rules for radar settings.

**Q: Does it work on Bedrock / mobile?**
A: No — **Java Edition only**. A separate Bedrock version exists but is not this mod.

**Q: Is it lightweight?**
A: Yes — only ~50-80 MB RAM overhead, one of the lightest minimap mods available.

**Q: What's the difference between Xaero's and JourneyMap?**
A: Xaero's is **lighter and faster**, with a cleaner vanilla-style look. JourneyMap has more features but uses more resources.

**Q: What Minecraft versions are supported?**
A: 1.18, 1.19, 1.20, 1.21, and 1.21+. Full list: 1.7.10 to 1.21+.

**Q: Fabric, Forge, or NeoForge?**
A: All three are supported.

**Q: Can I use it with shaders?**
A: Yes — fully compatible.

**Q: How do I uninstall it?**
A: Delete the `.jar` from `.minecraft/mods/` and restart.

**Q: Is there a "Fair Play" version?**
A: Yes — a Fair Play edition exists that disables entity radar for server compliance.

**Q: Why isn't my minimap showing?**
A: Check that the mod is in your mods folder, you're using the correct loader, and no conflicting mods are installed.

**Q: Does it work in multiplayer?**
A: Yes — it's designed for both singleplayer and multiplayer.

**Q: Can I share waypoints with friends?**
A: Waypoints are saved locally. Sharing requires manual coordinates.
